For days where your TSS is high or you have a "Threshold Range Surf" session:

Sourdough toast + Jam OR Oatmeal. No fats (avocado/PB) right before the ride as it slows absorption.

During Ride (Sugar Training)

If pushing for 280W, your body needs instant glycogen. Use electrolyte mix with carbs in your bottles. Practice this indoors now so your stomach can handle it at 13,000ft.

Protein Smoothie immediately. Your body is primed to absorb nutrients without storing them as fat.

🚨 Defeating the "Snack Attack"

You mentioned wanting to reduce crackers, cheese, and impulsive eating. Follow this sequence when stressed/hungry:

  1. Drink 16oz of water immediately. Thirst mimics hunger at altitude.
  2. If still hungry, eat high-volume, low-calorie first: Bowl of Edamame.
  3. Remove triggers: Do not keep crackers, cheese, or bars in the house.
