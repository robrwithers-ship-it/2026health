Fueling for Altitude & Sustained Power

Mount Blue Sky starts in Idaho Springs (7,500 ft) and climbs to over 14,100 ft. That is over 6,500 feet of relentless elevation gain. Sustained climbing combined with extreme altitude severely suppresses appetite but rapidly burns glycogen. We must train your gut to take in carbs at high sustained efforts.

🚴 The Blue Sky Prep Protocol

For days where your TSS is high or you have a "Threshold Range Surf" session:

Pre-Ride (Carb Focus)

Sourdough toast + Jam OR Oatmeal. No fats (avocado/PB) right before the ride as it slows absorption.

During Ride (Sugar Training)

Use electrolyte mix with carbs in your bottles. Practice this indoors now so your stomach can handle digesting sugar passing 12,000ft.

Post-Ride (Recovery)

Whey Protein Smoothie within 30-45 minutes. Mix in your 5g Afternoon Creatine dose here to leverage the insulin spike.

🚨 Defeating the "Snack Attack"

Following executive load or post-ride depletion, follow this sequence when stressed/hungry:

  1. Drink 16oz of water immediately. Altitude & A/C mask thirst as hunger.
  2. If still hungry, eat high-volume, low-calorie first: Bowl of Edamame.
  3. Remove triggers: Do not keep crackers, cheese, or bars in the house.
